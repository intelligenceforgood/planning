# 2026-01-05 Status Update

## Highlights

- Solidified storage architecture. Replaced temporary solutions and hacks. See details in [Storage Architecture](https://github.com/intelligenceforgood/core/blob/main/docs/design/storage.md)
- Implemented PII protection for victim intake data. See details in [PII Protection](https://github.com/intelligenceforgood/core/blob/main/docs/design/pii_vault.md)
  - Contact fields (reporter name, email, phone, handle) are Fernet-encrypted at intake time.

See [Performance Optimization: Case Classification](../tasks/perf_optimization_classification) for the mitigation plan.
